About Hallworks

Worker-first.
Built with the labor community.

Hallworks is an early-stage platform built on a direct principle: workers should be able to organize, join unions, and help run them.

Our starting point

Worker-first.
Open to collaboration.

We’re working toward a more connected experience for workers who want to organize or join a union, unions that want to support their members, and employers looking to work with union labor.

Hallworks is still taking shape. We’re listening, learning, and building carefully. We don’t claim to have every answer — the people closest to the work should help shape what comes next.
