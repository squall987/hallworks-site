Frequently asked questions

What is Hallworks?

Hallworks is an early-stage platform being built to help workers organize or find local unions, support union participation, and help employers connect with union labor.

Is Hallworks available now?

Not yet. The site introduces Hallworks and gathers early interest. The form requires email and role; phone and contact preference are optional. Submissions go to Formspree for early-access follow-up and do not create a Hallworks account or provide member operations.

Does Hallworks provide legal advice?

No. Hallworks does not provide legal advice or determine anyone’s rights or obligations. For advice about your specific situation, consult a qualified professional or appropriate labor organization.

How will my waitlist information be used?

The early-access form requires email and role and allows an optional phone number and contact preference. Formspree processes the submission so Hallworks can follow up about early access. Review the draft Privacy Policy for more information.

Can unions and employers share feedback?

Yes. Hallworks is interested in perspectives from workers, union organizers, unions, and employers as the platform is shaped. Use the early-access form to register your interest.
