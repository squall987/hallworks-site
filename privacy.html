Privacy policy · Draft

Privacy policy.
Draft for review.

DRAFT — FOR DISCUSSION ONLY. This policy requires review and revision by qualified privacy counsel before continued collection or phone outreach.

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Important: this is a draft requiring qualified privacy-counsel review. The early-access form requires email and role and collects optional phone number and contact preference. Formspree processes submissions for Hallworks early-access follow-up. Confirm all actual processing, retention, and opt-out practices before expanding collection.

1. Who we are and scope

This draft describes the proposed privacy practices of Hallworks (“Hallworks,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) for this website and related services. Hallworks is an early-stage project. This draft is not a representation that a particular business entity or service is currently operating.

2. Information we may collect

The early-access form requires your email address and role (worker, union organizer, or employer). You may optionally provide a phone number and contact preference (Call, Text, or Email only). Formspree processes submitted form fields and delivers them to Hallworks; the email, optional phone number, and contact preference are used only for one-to-one contact about early access. Phone numbers are never sold or used for bulk or marketing texts. Other future website services may collect additional information, which must be disclosed before collection.

3. Purposes and disclosure

Subject to applicable law and the disclosures provided at collection, information may be used to provide requested services, communicate about Hallworks, maintain security, and comply with legal obligations. We will not disclose or use personal information for materially different purposes without appropriate notice and choice where required. This draft does not establish any actual data sharing.

4. Sale, sharing, and sensitive information

Hallworks never sells the phone number you provide or uses it for bulk or marketing texts. The early-access form collects required email and role plus an optional phone number and contact preference; phone contact is one-to-one about early access only. Formspree processes submissions under its own terms. We do not ask for sensitive personal information through this prototype.

5. California privacy notice (CCPA/CPRA-aware draft)

California residents may have rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act, as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act, subject to applicable definitions, exceptions, and eligibility. Rights may include the right to know/access, correct, and delete personal information; the right to opt out of sale or sharing; the right to limit certain uses of sensitive personal information; and the right not to be discriminated against for exercising applicable rights.

Before a live collection service launches, Hallworks should provide required notices at or before collection, identify categories and purposes with sufficient specificity, establish a verifiable request process and authorized-agent process where required, respond within applicable timeframes, and provide a functional opt-out mechanism if applicable. This draft is not a complete notice at collection, and it does not confirm that any specific CCPA/CPRA obligation applies to Hallworks.

6. Retention, security, and children

If information is collected in a future service, we intend to retain it only as long as reasonably necessary for disclosed purposes and to use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards. No method of transmission or storage can be guaranteed secure. The prototype is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

7. Retention and opting out

Proposed retention: keep contact details only as long as needed to manage early-access follow-up; after an opt-out or the end of early-access outreach, delete or request deletion of the submission subject to applicable legal requirements. Confirm Formspree retention and deletion settings before launch. Reply STOP to a text to opt out of texts. Before collection continues, Hallworks must publish a clear way to opt out of calls and emails and request deletion.

If Hallworks ever adds automated or bulk messaging, this policy and the consent wording will be reviewed with counsel first.

Do not submit sensitive personal information through this prototype.

8. Changes and legal review

We may revise this draft as the service and its data practices develop. A final version should include an effective date and be reviewed by qualified counsel before publication or data collection.
