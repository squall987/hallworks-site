Choose with confidence

Questions to ask
any union software vendor.

Ask for a live walk-through of your workflows, not just a feature list.

  • Can members see dispatch position, call offers, and work history in the member app?
  • Which contract data is structured, and what still requires opening a separate document?
  • How are apprentice hours, evaluations, and wage-step changes recorded and reviewed?
  • Which roles—members, officers, training staff, and contractors—are included in the same product?
  • What does the price include? Ask about setup fees, seats, add-ons, and annual billing.
  • Is dues collection live or still in development? Can you demonstrate the complete money flow?
  • Which connections are live integrations, file import/export, or manual work?
  • What is available today, what is in development, and how can your local verify it?

Frequently asked

Know what is live.
Know what is next.

Do we have to replace the system our International provides?

No. Hallworks can be used alongside it. There are no live integrations with International systems; supported data movement is through file import/export rather than direct sync.

Are online dues payments available?

No. Online dues payments are in development and are not live. The app does not charge dues today.

Can our members use traveler dispatch?

Hallworks includes Book II traveler workflows, but sister-local coverage is still seed data, and traveler offers are not yet shown in the app. Confirm that the locals and workflows your members need are live before relying on them.

Do all union plans have the same features?

Yes. All published union tiers include all features and unlimited staff seats; the monthly price depends on member count. Annual billing includes two months free.

What does organizing cost?

Organizing is free until 90 days after recognition.

How is contractor pricing calculated?

Contractors with up to 3 active crew are free. Above 3 active crew, billing is $19 per company monthly plus one rate applied to every crew member: $5 for 4–25 crew, $4.50 for 26–100, or $4 for 101+.

Does Hallworks send bulk messages to members?

No. Segmented email, SMS, and push broadcasts are not available today.
